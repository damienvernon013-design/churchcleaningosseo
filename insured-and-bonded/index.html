Insured, Bonded and 22 Years Serving Osseo

The credentials your facilities committee or trustee board needs before authorising a cleaning contract.

When a church administrator is presenting a cleaning contractor to a facilities committee or trustee board, insurance and bonding are not optional items on the checklist. They are the first questions, and they should be. A cleaning crew working in a sanctuary or fellowship hall has unsupervised access to the building. Something that breaks, something that goes missing, or an injury to a crew member on your property creates liability exposure. Insurance and bonding are how that exposure is managed.

General liability insurance

Osseo Church Cleaning carries general liability insurance appropriate for commercial cleaning operations. Our coverage protects your organisation against claims arising from damage to property or bodily injury occurring during our cleaning activities. A certificate of insurance is provided with every written proposal, before any work begins.

Bonding

We maintain a janitorial bond. Bonding provides a layer of financial protection in the event of theft by a cleaning crew member. If your facilities trustee requires proof of bonding as a condition of contract, we supply it with the same documentation package as the insurance certificate.

Workers' compensation

Crew members are covered by workers' compensation insurance. If a crew member is injured while working in your facility, their medical costs and lost wages are covered under our policy, not yours. This matters particularly for organisations that might otherwise be exposed under volunteer or informal labour arrangements.

22 years in business

Insurers price commercial cleaning policies on claims history. A company that has been in business for 22 years and maintains current coverage has a track record that a new entrant does not. That history is part of what you are buying when you hire an established contractor rather than someone who started last year.
