What Does a Church Cleaning Service Actually Include?

What's included in your quote varies between providers. Know exactly what you're getting before you sign.

Not all church cleaning services are the same, and what's included in your quote varies widely between providers. Before your facilities committee signs a contract, you should know exactly what tasks are — and aren't — part of your standard service.

Standard inclusions in most church cleaning contracts

  • Vacuuming: Sanctuary carpet, including under pews and in corners
  • Hard floor mopping: Narthex tile, fellowship hall flooring, hallways
  • Trash removal: All bins emptied and relined with new bags
  • Restroom cleaning: Toilets, sinks, mirrors, floors scrubbed and disinfected; consumables restocked
  • Fellowship hall and kitchen: Counters, sink, serving area surfaces
  • Surface dusting: Pews, pulpit area, shelving, window ledges
  • Glass and mirrors: Narthex doors, interior glass partitions
  • Nursery and classroom sanitizing: Hard surfaces, changing areas, floors

Common add-ons (usually priced separately)

  • Carpet steam cleaning or extraction
  • Pew and chair upholstery cleaning
  • Floor stripping, waxing, and buffing
  • High dusting (light fixtures, ceiling beams, tops of shelving)
  • Post-event cleanup after weddings, funerals, or large community meals
  • Supply restocking (paper towels, hand soap, toilet paper) as a standing consumables package

What to ask your provider

When reviewing a quote, ask the provider to walk you through every task they will and won't perform. Request a written scope-of-work document rather than a verbal agreement. This protects your committee if quality ever falls short and gives you a clear basis for evaluating their performance visit to visit.

Customization is standard

Good church cleaning companies tailor their services to your building. If you have a nursery with specific health-department expectations, a sanctuary platform that should be treated with a particular product, or a fellowship hall kitchen used for community meals, tell your provider. A well-structured contract should reflect your building's actual needs, not a generic office-cleaning template.
