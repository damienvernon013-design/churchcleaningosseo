About Osseo Church Cleaning

22 years of experience cleaning faith facilities in Osseo and the northwest metro.

Osseo Church Cleaning specialises in cleaning and sanitizing faith facilities across Osseo, MN and the surrounding northwest metro communities. We have been doing this work for 22 years, which means we have cleaned through the seasonal rhythms that shape church calendars: Advent preparation, Lenten midweek services, Easter and Christmas high-attendance events, summer VBS periods, and the quieter weeks in between.

Our background is in faith facilities specifically, not general commercial cleaning. That distinction matters because a church administrator is not the same buyer as a facilities manager at a warehouse or an office park. The scheduling constraints are different, the surfaces are different, the sensitivity to disruption is different, and the relationship with the people who use the space is different.

How we work

Every engagement begins with an on-site walkthrough. We document the spaces included, the frequency of cleaning, the access windows, and any specific requirements—product restrictions, security procedures, key or code protocols. That document becomes the scope-of-work, and it is the benchmark against which you can measure every visit.

We assign crews by building rather than by route. The same people return to your facility each time, which reduces the learning curve and the chance of missed areas. New crew members are accompanied on their first several visits before working independently.

Coverage area

Osseo sits at the crossing of Hennepin County Road 81 and Highway 81 in the northwest metro. Our 25-mile service radius from Osseo covers communities to the north including Dayton (approximately 12 miles via County Road 81) and Rogers (approximately 14 miles via Highway 101), to the east including Brooklyn Park and Brooklyn Center, and south to Plymouth, which lies roughly 10 miles down Highway 169.

Who manages the account

Your primary point of contact is the same person throughout the relationship. Scheduling changes, scope adjustments and concerns go to one person, not a call centre. Responses to messages left during business hours are the same business day; after-hours messages receive a response by 10am the following business day.
