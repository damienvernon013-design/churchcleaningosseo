Our Church Cleaning Services in Osseo

Three specialised service lines covering every space in a faith facility, scheduled around your worship calendar.

Osseo Church Cleaning serves congregations across Osseo and the northwest metro with cleaning programmes tailored to faith facilities. The three core services below address the spaces that generate the most work—and the spaces where a cleaning failure is most visible to the congregation.

All services begin with a scope walkthrough. We document what is cleaned, how often, and on what schedule. That written scope is your benchmark for holding us accountable on every visit.

Sanctuary & Worship Space Cleaning

Pre- and post-service cleaning of sanctuaries, chapels, chancel areas and narthex. Scheduled to the minute around your Sunday bulletin and midweek programmes.

Fellowship Hall & Kitchen Cleaning

Post-event cleaning of multipurpose halls, commercial kitchen surfaces, serving areas and storage. Turnaround cleaning between back-to-back events available.

Nursery & Classroom Sanitizing

Child-safe disinfection of nurseries, Sunday school classrooms and VBS spaces. Hard surfaces, toys, changing areas and flooring addressed on a fixed schedule.

How we price our services

Pricing depends on square footage, the number of spaces included, cleaning frequency, and access logistics specific to your building. We do not quote by phone without seeing the facility. A scope walkthrough takes 30–45 minutes and produces a written proposal within two business days.
