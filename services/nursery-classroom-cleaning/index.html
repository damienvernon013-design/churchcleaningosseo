Nursery & Classroom Sanitizing in Osseo, MN

Child-safe disinfection of nurseries, Sunday school rooms and VBS spaces using appropriate products and documented procedures.

Nurseries and children's ministry spaces carry health risks that other church spaces do not. Infants share surfaces, toys and changing equipment. Young children touch floors and then their faces. Respiratory illnesses cycle through congregations via the nursery faster than through any other space in the building. Parents and trustees know this, and a documented cleaning procedure for the nursery is increasingly what families ask about before they enroll.

Osseo Church Cleaning cleans nurseries and classrooms with products appropriate for use in spaces where children are present. We follow a documented procedure for each visit and can provide cleaning logs on request.

What we address in nurseries

Hard toy sanitizing, crib rail and mattress wipe-down, changing table disinfection with barrier replacement, floor vacuuming and mopping, diaper pail servicing, sink cleaning, window ledge and door handle disinfection, waste removal.

Sunday school and classroom spaces

Chair and table wiping, floor sweeping and mopping, supply shelf and storage cube dusting, waste removal, restroom servicing if directly adjacent to the classroom. For VBS periods or high-use summer programmes, we can increase frequency temporarily without altering the annual contract.

Products and safety

We use EPA-registered disinfectants with appropriate dwell times for the surfaces being cleaned. We do not use products from the unsafe-for-children category on surfaces where infants will have contact. If your facility has specific product restrictions, note them at the scope walkthrough and we will accommodate them or flag where we cannot.

Protect the families in your care

A written cleaning scope for your nursery is a demonstrable commitment to the families who trust you with their children. Call (866) 958-8773 to schedule a walkthrough.
