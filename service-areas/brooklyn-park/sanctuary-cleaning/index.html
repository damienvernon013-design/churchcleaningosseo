Sanctuary & Worship Space Cleaning in Brooklyn Park, MN

Sanctuary cleaning for Brooklyn Park congregations, including multi-service facilities and converted commercial spaces.

An East African Christian congregation on West Broadway in Brooklyn Park holds three services on Sunday: a 7am Amharic-language service, a 10am English service, and a 1pm combined service. The sanctuary—a converted retail building of approximately 3,800 sq ft with tile floors, folding chairs and a raised platform area—must be cleaned between the 7am and 10am services in a 45-minute window.

We clean the facility Saturday evening for the baseline reset and return Sunday morning at 8am for the between-service turnaround: platform swept, chairs realigned, microphone stands repositioned per their layout, floors spot-mopped at the entrance where track-in concentrates, restrooms checked and restocked. The scope was built around their specific Sunday sequence, not around a generic church template.

Brooklyn Park's diverse congregation base means we have experience working around multi-language service schedules, facilities in converted retail footprints, and buildings where the seating configuration changes between uses. We document all of it.
