Fellowship Hall & Kitchen Cleaning in Champlin, MN

Post-event hall and kitchen cleaning for Champlin faith facilities along the Elm Creek and Highway 169 corridor.

A Baptist congregation in Champlin hosts a weekly meal for seniors through a county-partnered programme, serving approximately 40 to 60 participants each Tuesday. The fellowship hall seats the meal, and the kitchen serves a full hot lunch. Because the programme receives county referrals, the facility is subject to periodic county health department inspection, and the kitchen logs are part of what inspectors review.

We clean Tuesday afternoons following the meal close. The scope includes kitchen log completion (date, products used, areas cleaned, crew initials), which we provide as a form that can be produced for inspection. Hall floors, table service and restroom are included. The congregation's kitchen is a residential-style kitchen in a fellowship hall—not a commercial kitchen—which means different product considerations than a full commercial installation.
