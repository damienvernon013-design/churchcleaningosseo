Fellowship Hall & Kitchen Cleaning in Brooklyn Center, MN

Post-event hall and kitchen cleaning for Brooklyn Center faith communities, including high-volume community meal programmes.

A congregation in the Brookdale area of Brooklyn Center operates a food shelf and community meal programme that serves 120 to 150 guests on Friday evenings. The fellowship hall seats the dining portion, and the kitchen—a full commercial installation added in 2008 with a six-burner commercial range, commercial hood, and three-compartment sink—produces a hot meal for the entire count. Friday evening cleaning follows the close of service at approximately 8:30pm.

The cleaning scope for this facility is one of the more detailed in our portfolio. Commercial range cleaning with a degreaser appropriate for the specific grate finish, hood filter removal and grease trap check, three-compartment sink scouring, commercial dishwasher rack cleaning, floor mopping with a degreasing product on the quarry tile, and a full hall reset. Because this is a food-service operation serving vulnerable populations, we follow the documentation protocol and provide cleaning logs that the congregation can present to their health department liaison.

Brooklyn Center community meal programmes operate at a scale that requires commercial cleaning experience, not a residential-style clean. We have the equipment and the products to handle the volume.
