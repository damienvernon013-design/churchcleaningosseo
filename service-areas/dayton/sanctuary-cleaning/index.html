Sanctuary & Worship Space Cleaning in Dayton, MN

Sanctuary cleaning for Dayton congregations in a rapidly growing community.

A nondenominational congregation in Dayton's newer residential growth area near the Crow River occupies a purpose-built facility completed in 2021. The 6,000 sq ft sanctuary has polished concrete floors throughout, a high-bay ceiling with exposed truss structure, and a platform area with AV equipment permanently installed. The polished concrete is sealed and requires different maintenance than traditional tile or carpet: dust mopping before any wet mopping, a pH-neutral cleaner, and immediate attention to any liquid spills before they reach grout lines at expansion joints.

The facility holds Sunday services at 9am and 11am, with a youth group Wednesday evening that uses the sanctuary for large-group time. We clean Monday mornings for the thorough weekly clean and check the sanctuary Wednesday evening after youth group before locking up. The Wednesday check is a 20-minute walk-through rather than a full clean, addressing whatever the evening left behind.
