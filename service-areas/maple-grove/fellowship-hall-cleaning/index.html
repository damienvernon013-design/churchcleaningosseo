Fellowship Hall & Kitchen Cleaning in Maple Grove, MN

Post-event hall and kitchen cleaning for Maple Grove faith facilities.

A nondenominational congregation near the Arbor Lakes shopping district in Maple Grove operates a 4,200 sq ft fellowship hall that serves as a community rental space three to four evenings per week. The hall is booked by local scouting groups, a homeschool cooperative, and private rental parties. Post-rental cleaning is the congregation's responsibility under their rental agreement, and their volunteer team was struggling to keep up.

We clean the hall on Monday, Wednesday and Friday evenings following rental events, and do a full kitchen clean on Monday mornings after the Sunday brunch programme. The scope documents the specific sequence: floors swept before mopping, tables surface-cleaned and stacked to one wall, chairs folded and racked, kitchen sinks and counters sanitized, restrooms serviced, waste removed. The rental coordinator has our contact number and can add a clean if a weekend event runs over.

Rental venues within church facilities

Maple Grove faith facilities that rent to outside groups often find that post-rental cleaning is the hardest operational piece to manage. We can be the post-rental cleaner of record, which simplifies your rental agreements and removes the dependency on available volunteers.
