Sanctuary & Worship Space Cleaning in Maple Grove, MN

Scheduled sanctuary cleaning for Maple Grove faith communities.

A congregation along County Road 101 in Maple Grove recently asked us to take over sanctuary cleaning after their long-time volunteer custodian retired. The facility seats approximately 400 in the sanctuary, with an additional overflow room connected by audio feed. The challenge was a stone tile floor in the narthex that had been treated incorrectly with a general-purpose cleaner, leaving a film that was visibly dull under the lobby lighting.

We scoped the facility, identified the appropriate stone-safe neutral cleaner, and included a restorative clean of the narthex floor as a first-visit item at no additional charge. The sanctuary itself runs pre-service on Sunday mornings and a thorough clean on Monday. The overflow room is addressed only on weeks when it was opened for attendance.

That kind of specificity—which spaces, which days, which procedures—is what the written scope documents. Maple Grove congregations with similarly complex facilities benefit from the same approach.

Maple Grove sanctuary types we clean

Traditional worship spaces with fixed pews, contemporary sanctuaries with upholstered chairs in moveable configurations, chapel spaces attached to larger campuses, and overflow rooms used on high-attendance Sundays and holidays.
