Fellowship Hall & Kitchen Cleaning in Corcoran, MN

Post-event hall and kitchen cleaning for Corcoran rural faith facilities.

A Catholic parish in the Corcoran area serves a rural territory that extends into neighbouring Medina and Loretto townships. The parish hall hosts a monthly breakfast after the 10am Sunday mass, drawing 60 to 80 attendees. The kitchen is a large residential installation that has been modified over the years: a commercial-grade range was added in the 1990s, a commercial dishwasher in 2010, but the countertops and cabinetry are original 1970s construction.

Post-breakfast cleaning covers the commercial range and dishwasher with appropriate products, counter wipe-down with a food-safe sanitizer, floor mopping (linoleum tile), and hall floor sweeping and mopping following table and chair breakdown. Because the parish serves a rural area, the monthly breakfast is a significant social event and the hall is typically used for 3 to 4 hours straight, generating more floor soil than a shorter event would.

Rural Corcoran facilities often have mixed kitchen equipment spanning multiple decades. We note the specific equipment at the scope walkthrough and ensure we bring the right products for each surface type.
