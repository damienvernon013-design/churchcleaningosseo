Sanctuary & Worship Space Cleaning in Rogers, MN

Sanctuary cleaning for Rogers congregations, from historic Main Street facilities to newer I-94 corridor churches.

An evangelical congregation near the 141st Avenue industrial corridor in Rogers holds services Saturday evening and Sunday morning to accommodate congregation members who work rotating shifts at the industrial facilities along I-94 North. Saturday evening services end around 8pm; Sunday morning starts at 9am. The between-service window is 13 hours, which allows for a thorough Saturday post-service clean before the Sunday morning congregation arrives.

The sanctuary is a converted industrial building of approximately 8,500 sq ft with polished concrete floors, exposed HVAC ductwork at ceiling level, and a stage platform with permanent instrument and AV installation. The cleaning scope includes the stage area (swept but not wet-mopped to protect the equipment mounts), main floor wet-mopped with a commercial neutral cleaner, restrooms serviced, lobby and entryway addressed, folding chairs repositioned to the Sunday configuration after Saturday evening breakdown.

Rogers facilities in the industrial conversion category share certain characteristics: high ceilings that collect dust above standard reach, irregular floor plans, and parking areas that track in more grit than a residential-adjacent suburban church.
