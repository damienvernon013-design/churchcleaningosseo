Why Osseo Buildings Choose Us

The factors that matter to a facilities trustee or church administrator evaluating cleaning contractors.

Most cleaning contractors in the northwest metro are not specialised in faith facilities. They clean offices, retail spaces and warehouses, and they apply the same protocols to a sanctuary that they use in a break room. The differences that matter to a church—schedule sensitivity, surface diversity, volunteer-crew dynamics, and the particular relationship between a building and the people who use it—are not things a general contractor is set up to manage.

22 years specifically in faith facility cleaning

We have cleaned churches, chapels, fellowship halls and ministry buildings for 22 years. That history means we have encountered every common scheduling conflict, every unusual surface, and every access-logistics problem that a church building presents. We do not learn on your facility.

Written scope before work begins

Every engagement begins with a documented scope-of-work. The scope lists every space, every task, and the cleaning frequency. You know exactly what you are paying for, and you have a document to reference if something is missed.

Assigned crews, not rotating strangers

The same people clean your building every visit. Assigned crews learn the quirks of your facility—the pew that needs extra attention, the fellowship hall floor that shows scuff marks, the nursery restroom where the mop head catches on the door frame. That institutional knowledge does not accumulate with rotating crews.

Insured and bonded

We carry general liability insurance and bonding appropriate for commercial cleaning operations. A certificate of insurance is included with every written proposal. See our insured and bonded page for details.

Honest pricing, no hidden minimums

Our proposals are line-itemed. You see what each space costs and can adjust scope before signing. We do not add service minimums or travel fees after the proposal is accepted. See the pricing page for what drives the cost.
